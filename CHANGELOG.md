# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [0.9.0] - first public release

### Added
- A beaver mascot in the Explorer sidebar who says "hello :)" on start and "bye :)" on shutdown.
- Typing and backspacing make him chew a tree, haul logs, and build a dam. A pond rises behind the dam,
  a lodge appears halfway, baby beavers wade around it, and birds and a rare otter drop by.
- The tree gets a deeper chewed waist with every log, starts to lean, and topples when the dam is finished.
- The camera pans and zooms out automatically as the dam grows.
- A **World** tab with a map of 30 streams and 4 big rivers. Every finished dam restores a stream, brings its swamp
  back to life and turns dry land into meadow. The map can also be opened in an editor tab.
- Idle behaviour: slow progress while the window is focused but you are not typing, and now and then a rest on top of the dam.
- Day and night that follow your clock (or a fast cycle, or a manual switch), with sun, moon, stars and fireflies.
- Cheers (`yay :)`) when code runs successfully (Positron console and scripts, notebook cells, terminal commands, tasks)
  and `oops :(` when a run fails.
- A heart when the mouse pointer is near the beaver.
- Settings, commands and a run-detection log (`Beaver: Show run detection log`).
